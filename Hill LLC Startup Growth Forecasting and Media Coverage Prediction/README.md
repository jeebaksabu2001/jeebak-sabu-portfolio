# 🚀 Hill LLC Startup Growth Forecasting and Media Coverage Prediction

## 📌 Project Summary

This project analyses startup growth data for Hill LLC Inc. from the perspective of a data scientist working for a venture capital investment firm. The main goal is to predict the company’s future payroll, revenue, and media coverage risk over the next 12 months.

The project was completed using Python in Jupyter Notebook and includes data visualisation, regression modelling, robust regression, log-transformed revenue modelling, logistic regression, model evaluation, and business interpretation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Forecast Hill LLC’s payroll 12 months into the future.
- Predict future revenue using regression models.
- Estimate the likelihood of negative media coverage.
- Compare model performance and reliability.
- Provide investment-focused recommendations.

## 📊 Dataset Used

The dataset used in this project is `hill_llc_startup_data.csv`.

It contains 24 months of company-level data related to payroll, revenue, scandals, and media coverage.

## 🧾 Dataset Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `Months_Since_Launch` | Number of months since the company was launched |
| `Payroll` | Total payroll amount for the month |
| `Revenue` | Total revenue generated by the company |
| `Base_10_Log_Revenue` | Base-10 logarithm of revenue used for modelling |
| `Scandals` | Number of scandals recorded in the month |
| `Media Coverage` | Media sentiment, recorded as positive or negative |

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Import and Inspection

The dataset was loaded into Python and inspected to understand the available variables, data structure, and modelling requirements.

### 2. Payroll Trend Analysis

A line plot was created to visualise payroll over time.

The analysis showed that payroll generally increased over the 24-month period. However, one visible outlier appeared around month 10, possibly caused by a one-time event such as bonus payments, hiring costs, or restructuring expenses.

### 3. Payroll Prediction

Two models were trained to predict payroll:

- Ordinary Least Squares Linear Regression
- Huber Regressor

Mean Absolute Error was used to evaluate model performance.

| Model | Result |
|---|---:|
| OLS Validation MAE | Approximately $1.51M |
| Huber Validation MAE | Approximately $602,918 |
| Huber Test MAE | Approximately $258,873 |

The Huber Regressor performed better because it was more robust to the payroll outlier.

### 4. Payroll Forecast

Using the Huber regression model, payroll was forecasted 12 months into the future.

Estimated payroll at month 36:

| Forecast | Value |
|---|---:|
| Predicted Payroll | Approximately $16.39M |
| Lower Estimate | Approximately $15.20M |
| Upper Estimate | Approximately $17.57M |

### 5. Revenue Modelling

Revenue was modelled using two approaches:

- Linear Regression on original revenue
- Linear Regression on log-transformed revenue

| Model | Validation MAE |
|---|---:|
| Original Revenue Model | Approximately $112,214 |
| Log-Transformed Revenue Model | Approximately $10,694 |

The log-transformed model performed better in short-term prediction, but it produced unrealistic results when extended too far into the future.

### 6. Revenue Forecast

The models were used to predict revenue 12 months ahead.

| Model | 12-Month Revenue Prediction |
|---|---:|
| Original Revenue Model | Approximately $1.41M |
| Log-Transformed Revenue Model | Approximately $12.84M |

The large difference between the two forecasts shows the importance of understanding model assumptions, especially when predicting startup growth.

### 7. Media Coverage Prediction

A Logistic Regression model was used to predict whether future media coverage would be positive or negative.

Features used included:

- Revenue
- Payroll
- Months Since Launch

The `Scandals` variable was not useful because no scandals were recorded in the dataset.

The model predicted a very low probability of negative media coverage in the next 12 months, assuming no scandals occur.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Payroll showed a clear upward trend over time.
- A major payroll outlier was identified around month 10.
- Huber Regression performed better than ordinary Linear Regression for payroll prediction.
- Predicted payroll at month 36 was approximately $16.39M.
- Log-transformed revenue modelling performed better for short-term prediction.
- Long-term revenue forecasting using the log model became unrealistic.
- Logistic Regression predicted a low likelihood of negative media coverage.
- The company appears to show positive growth potential based on the available data.

## 💡 Business Recommendation

Hill LLC Inc. appears to show signs of growth, with increasing payroll and revenue potential. From an investment perspective, the company may be worth further investigation.

However, the predictions should not be used alone. The dataset only contains 24 months of data, and startup forecasts can be highly uncertain. Before making an investment decision, the venture capital firm should also review market conditions, competition, cash flow, operating costs, leadership quality, customer growth, and external business risks.

## ⚠️ Model Limitations

- The dataset is small, with only 24 months of observations.
- Payroll contained an outlier that affected ordinary regression.
- Long-term forecasts may be unreliable because startup growth does not continue forever at the same rate.
- The media coverage model may be limited because no scandals were recorded.
- External factors such as competition, regulation, market demand, and economic conditions were not included.
