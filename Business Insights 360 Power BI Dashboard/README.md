# Business Insights 360 Power BI Dashboard

## Project Summary

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a complete 360-degree business performance view across finance, sales, marketing, supply chain, and executive-level decision-making.

The dashboard consolidates multiple business functions into one reporting solution, allowing users to analyse revenue, profitability, gross margin, forecast accuracy, customer performance, product performance, regional performance, and market share trends.

The main purpose of this project is to demonstrate how Power BI can be used to transform business data into a professional, interactive, and insight-driven business intelligence dashboard.

## Business Problem

A business with multiple products, customers, regions, and channels needs a clear way to track performance across departments. Looking at finance, sales, marketing, and supply chain data separately can make it difficult for managers to understand the full business picture.

The business needs a dashboard that can answer questions such as:

- How much revenue is the company generating?
- Is the business profitable?
- Which regions, customers, products, and divisions drive performance?
- How does actual performance compare with benchmarks such as last year or target?
- Which customers and products are performing well or poorly?
- How accurate is demand forecasting?
- Which markets or subzones need management attention?

This Power BI dashboard solves that problem by bringing all key business views into one interactive reporting solution.

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this dashboard are to:

1. Build a multi-page Power BI dashboard for business performance monitoring.
2. Track key financial metrics such as net sales, gross margin, and net profit.
3. Analyse customer and product performance.
4. Compare actual performance against benchmarks such as last year and target.
5. Monitor forecast accuracy and supply chain risk.
6. Identify regional, market, customer, and product-level performance gaps.
7. Provide an executive-level summary for strategic decision-making.

## Key Insights

- Net Sales reached approximately **$3.34bn**.
- Gross Margin % was approximately **30.85%**.
- Net Profit % was negative at approximately **-21.36%**.
- Forecast Accuracy was approximately **81.17%**.
- PC division generated the largest share of revenue.
- Retailer channel contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Some product segments generated strong revenue but weak profitability.
- Regional and subzone performance varied significantly.
- Forecasting performance was reasonable overall, but net error and risk indicators show opportunities for supply chain improvement.
- Executive-level analysis shows that revenue growth must be supported by stronger profitability management.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following recommendations are suggested:

1. **Improve profitability**
   - Investigate the main reasons behind negative Net Profit %.
   - Review operating expenses, cost of goods sold, freight costs, and post-invoice deductions.

2. **Focus on high-revenue divisions**
   - Since the PC division contributes the largest revenue share, management should continue tracking its performance closely.
   - Product-level profitability should be monitored to ensure high sales also produce healthy profit.

3. **Strengthen margin management**
   - Gross Margin % should be tracked by customer, product, region, and channel.
   - Low-margin customers or products should be reviewed for pricing, discounting, or cost issues.

4. **Improve supply chain forecasting**
   - Forecast errors should be analysed by customer and product.
   - Products or customers with high forecast error should receive more attention in demand planning.

5. **Review regional performance**
   - Subzones or regions with negative Net Profit % should be investigated.
   - Management should identify whether the issue is low gross margin, high expenses, weak sales, or poor forecast accuracy.

6. **Optimise channel strategy**
   - Retailer is the largest revenue channel, so it should remain a key focus.
   - Direct and Distributor channels can be reviewed for growth opportunities and profitability improvement.

7. **Use executive dashboard for decision-making**
   - Senior leaders can use the Executive View to monitor overall health and quickly identify areas needing attention.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to build a complete business intelligence solution across multiple business functions. The dashboard brings together finance, sales, marketing, supply chain, and executive reporting into one interactive report.

The analysis shows that while the business generates strong net sales, profitability remains a major concern due to negative Net Profit %. The dashboard helps identify where management should focus attention, including cost control, margin improvement, product profitability, supply chain forecasting, regional performance, and channel strategy.

Overall, the Business Insights 360 dashboard provides a practical example of how data visualisation and business intelligence can support strategic decision-making.
